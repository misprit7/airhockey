How it was made

  1. A simulation-only physics teacher (expert_player.py) that sees the same delayed frames and drives the same decoder, guard and thermal model: minimum-time blocks on the line with the most time margin, banks forecast under the rail-restitution spread, deep load-scheduled guard, contact-normal strikes, and rail pucks left alone when the drives are hot.
  2. DAgger into production's network widened to 1536: the teacher labels defense and guard states; production's own action labels its slow-puck attacks (the teacher's precise strikes did not survive imitation).
  3. PPO with the trainer's new regime (adaptive learning rate from 1e-6, std floor, critic warm-up) and teacher regularisation on defense: 51M steps, then 80M with a 30/s possession-delay cost after the first fine-tune learned to hold the puck.

Known weaknesses: one-timers off fast incoming pucks; slow pucks pinned at a rail or back corner can sit, especially when the drives are hot; the sim still uses the historical paddle contact (the table-measured one is opt-in). The proxy is somewhat easier than the real person (production's inferred table share ~58% vs 71% here): read the gap, not the level.
