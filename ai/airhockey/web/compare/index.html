How the comparison works

Each segment starts from measured puck and robot positions. Initial velocities use the preceding 40 ms of measurements; unavailable velocities and initial profile acceleration start at zero.

After that, only recorded robot commands and the measured human paddle enter the simulator. The puck and robot are never corrected to the recording.

The environment’s physics and firmware motion profile run without training resets. A segment ends at a simulated goal, a recorded stop, or a human tracking gap over 150 ms. Missing camera measurements are hidden.

Positive values apply commands later. Changing this reruns the grid.
